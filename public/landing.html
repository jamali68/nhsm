Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Does NH POS work without internet?

Yes, 100%! All your bills, customers, and stock are saved locally on your device. Whenever internet is available, it automatically syncs with the secure cloud.

Can I install it on my phone?

Yes! NH POS is a Progressive Web App (PWA). Just open Chrome or Safari, click 'Install App' or 'Add to Home Screen', and it runs like a regular mobile app.

Is the Free Plan really free forever?

Yes, our Free Starter plan is lifetime free with up to 100 inventory items and 50 khata customers. No credit card is required to sign up.

Which printers are supported?

All standard USB and Bluetooth thermal receipt printers (58mm and 80mm ESC/POS) as well as regular desktop A4/A5 laser and inkjet printers.
