Shift Timetable & Usage Rules

1.
Two Fixed Shifts Daily: Shift 1 operates from 12:00 AM to 12:00 PM PKT. Shift 2 operates from 12:00 PM to 12:00 AM PKT.
2.
1 Manual Refresh Allowed Per Shift: You can fetch updated stock and customer lists once per shift. If already refreshed, the system informs you that the shift limit is reached.
3.
Offline Safety Guard: If the device is offline, the refresh button will ask you to connect to the internet first. Offline billing and stock lookups continue uninterrupted from local storage.
4.
Order Booker Synchronization: Orders placed in the field are kept locally on the booker's device until connected online, ensuring safe transmission to the cloud.
